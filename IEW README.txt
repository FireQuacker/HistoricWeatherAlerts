# 🚨 NWS Historical Heat Advisory Checker

A clean, Python-based tool designed to query the **Iowa Environmental Mesonet (IEM) API** to determine if the National Weather Service (NWS) had active Heat Advisories or Excessive Heat Warnings/Watches for a specific U.S. location on a given historical date.

This tool acts as a critical compliance and audit mechanism for verifying environmental hazards.
